INTERPRETATION FRAMEWORK
------------------------
A calibration slope below 1 indicates prediction compression: the model reproduces the central range more strongly than the extreme observed values.
Regional residual patterns identify conditional prediction bias that can be hidden by a near-zero overall mean residual.
High error in a particular solubility region does not by itself establish a causal mechanism.
Compound-level calibration summarizes repeated scaffold-aware held-out predictions rather than fitted training predictions.
