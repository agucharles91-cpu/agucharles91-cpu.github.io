SCRIPT 15 — INCREMENTAL RING MODEL
======================================================================

OBJECTIVE
Test whether ring-related descriptors add explanatory power beyond the canonical MolWt + MolLogP baseline.

POPULATION
Population C = 8,643 compounds.
Population C was taken directly from the established standard_analytical_domain flag in molecular_features.csv.

MODELS
M0: Solubility ~ rdkit_molwt + rdkit_mollogp
M1: M0 + rdkit_ring_count
M2: M0 + rdkit_aromatic_rings
M3: M0 + rdkit_ring_count + rdkit_aromatic_rings

INTERPRETATION CAUTION
This is an explanatory analysis rather than a held-out prediction evaluation.
Ring count is not equivalent to fused-ring count.
Ring count is not a direct measurement of crystal packing, lattice energy or melting point.
A statistically significant ring coefficient does not by itself establish a causal ring effect.
