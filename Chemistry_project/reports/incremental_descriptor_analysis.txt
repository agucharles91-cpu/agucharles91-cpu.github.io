METHODOLOGICAL NOTES
HC3 robust covariance is used for individual coefficient inference.
Nested F-tests use conventional OLS covariance.
HeavyAtomCount, MolMR and LabuteASA were not included in the initial candidate screen because Script 07 established their strong correlation with MolWt.
Ring count is not equivalent to fused-ring count.
This analysis is explanatory and does not provide held-out generalization performance.
