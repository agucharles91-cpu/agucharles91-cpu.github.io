INTERPRETATION FRAMEWORK
------------------------
The analysis should be interpreted as evidence of error heterogeneity rather than evidence of causation. Descriptor regions with higher MAE indicate areas where the current six-descriptor Gradient Boosting representation has greater predictive difficulty. Differences in mean residual indicate conditional directional bias within those regions.

Particular attention should be given to regions where:
1. compound count is sufficiently large;
2. MAE is substantially above the overall MAE;
3. residual direction is consistently positive or negative;
4. the result is supported by more than one descriptor;
5. scaffold frequency suggests limited structural representation.
