METHODOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The purpose of this analysis is to determine whether the structural-model improvement observed in the original scaffold-aware split is stable across multiple independent scaffold-level holdouts.
Each repetition keeps entire Bemis-Murcko scaffold groups together, preventing the same scaffold from appearing in both training and test data.
Positive M4 delta R-squared indicates that M4 explains more held-out variance than the MolWt + MolLogP baseline.
Negative M4 delta RMSE or MAE indicates improved prediction error relative to the baseline.
These results should be interpreted as robustness evidence, not as a final estimate of universal model performance.
