Positioning

Engineering the layer between data and decisions

My work sits at the intersection of analytics engineering, data analysis, business intelligence, and applied data science.

I am most interested in the part of analytics that is often invisible in the final dashboard: understanding the source data, designing reliable transformations, modelling the data correctly, validating the result, and choosing an analytical method that actually fits the question.

My background in Chemistry also influences how I approach analytical problems. I tend to start with the measurement and underlying process, rather than beginning with a preferred visualisation or model.

Data systems
ETL/ELT pipelines, SQL, PostgreSQL, Snowflake, DuckDB, dbt and analytical data modelling.
Analytical work
Exploratory analysis, cohort analysis, longitudinal analysis, statistical modelling and applied machine learning.
Decision support
Power BI, Tableau and reproducible analytical reporting designed to communicate evidence rather than simply display data.
Scientific perspective
A chemistry background combined with ongoing postgraduate training in data science.
